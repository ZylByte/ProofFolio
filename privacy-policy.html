Information the app handles

ProofFolio lets you create and store the following. You decide what to add.

  • Projects, events, notes, checklists and reports you write or generate.
  • People profiles, which may include names and other details you enter about other people.
  • Attachments: photos, videos, audio, documents, notes and saved webpages that you import, capture or save.
  • Private Vault content: sensitive files you choose to store in the separate, password-protected area.

If you store details about other people, please make sure you have a good reason and, where needed, their permission to keep them.

Local storage and offline use

ProofFolio is designed to work offline. You can create and organize projects, people, events, notes and checklists without relying on a constant internet connection.

The information you create is stored on your device. [Confirm: app-private storage, copies of imported files, or links to files elsewhere on the device.]

Some things need a connection by their nature, such as loading a webpage or following a link. When you load a webpage, your device contacts that website directly, and its own privacy practices apply.

Working offline does not by itself mean that no data ever leaves your device. Whether any data is sent, synchronized, backed up or shared depends on the points below.

Developer to confirm:

  • Whether the app syncs or uploads anything to a server you or a provider operate.
  • Whether Android backup is allowed (android:allowBackup and backup rules in the manifest). If so, app data may be backed up to the user's Google account by Android, and this policy should say so.
  • Whether saved webpages are downloaded by the app and how they are stored.

Retention and deletion

You can delete projects, people profiles, attachments, Vault items and other content inside the app. Content you delete is removed from the app's storage on your device. Copies you exported, shared or backed up elsewhere are not removed.
