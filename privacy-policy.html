About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ZylByte ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the ProofFolio mobile application for Android (package name: com.zylbyte.prooffolio) (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Summary

  • ProofFolio is an offline-first app. The content you create in the App stays on your device.
  • No account is required. We do not ask you to sign up, log in, or give us your name or email address.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your content. We cannot see, access, or recover your people profiles, case files, notes, checklists, links, photos, videos, audio, documents, or PDFs.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or behavior-tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.
  • The App uses the internet for only two purposes: subscription management and purchases for the optional Premium plan, and converting a web page that you choose into a PDF.

Information Stored on Your Device (Not Sent to Us)

ProofFolio helps you organize personal records. Everything below is entered or imported by you and is stored locally on your device only:

  • People profiles: name, phone number, email address, website/URL, notes, status label, tags, and an optional profile photo;
  • Case files: title, description, date and time, location (as text you type), status, priority, tags, favorites, and an activity timeline;
  • Evidence items attached to case files: photos, videos, audio files, PDFs and other documents, web links, notes, and checklists, including photos and videos you capture with your device camera;
  • Private Vault: photos and videos you move or copy into the Vault, and their thumbnails;
  • Generated files: case report PDFs and web pages you convert to PDF, along with the web address, page title, and capture time saved with them;
  • Security and app settings: your PIN, biometric preference, recovery key information, and which areas of the App you have locked.

How this data is protected: Text-based records (such as people, case files, notes, checklists, and links) are kept in a local database encrypted with SQLCipher. The encryption key is kept in your device's secure, hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore). Photos, videos, audio files, and documents are kept in the App's private storage area, which other apps cannot access under Android's security model; these files are protected by the Android app sandbox and by your device's own encryption and screen lock.

None of this data is transmitted to us or to any server we operate. Because we never receive it, we cannot access it, restore it, or recover it for you if you lose your device, uninstall the App, or forget your PIN and recovery key.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any content you store in the App. The limited information that is processed through third-party services when you use certain features is described below.

Subscription management (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

The App offers an optional Premium subscription (quarterly or yearly) that removes the limits on the number of people profiles and case files. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. To manage and verify subscription status, the App uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service that acts as our service provider.

When your device is online, the RevenueCat SDK in the App may contact RevenueCat's servers to check subscription status, to load the available plans and prices, and to complete or restore a purchase. This can happen even if you have never purchased Premium. The information involved may include:

  • An anonymous, randomly generated app user ID created by RevenueCat (we do not link it to your name or email);
  • Purchase and subscription details (product, price tier, purchase and renewal/expiry dates, subscription status, and Google Play purchase tokens/transaction identifiers);
  • Basic technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings, and IP address (which RevenueCat uses to deliver the service and prevent fraud).

The App does not enable collection of your advertising ID. We do not receive your payment card details or Google account password; payments are handled entirely by Google Play. Subscription status is cached on your device, so Premium features keep working when you are offline.

Converting a web page to PDF ("URL to PDF")

If you use this feature, you enter a web address (http or https). The App then:

  • performs a quick connectivity check (a domain-name lookup) to see whether you are online;
  • loads the page you entered in an in-app web view, much like a web browser. JavaScript is enabled so that pages display correctly, and the web view may store cookies and website data on your device as a browser would;
  • renders the page into a PDF and saves it in the App's private storage on your device, together with the web address, page title, and capture time.

Your device connects directly to the website you chose and to any third-party content that page loads (such as images, scripts, fonts, advertising or analytics services). Those websites and services may receive your IP address and standard device/browser information and may set cookies, under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. We do not receive the web address you enter or the resulting PDF.

Please only convert pages that you are permitted to save, and review the privacy practices of the websites you visit.

Information handled by Google

If you download or update the App from Google Play, or purchase a subscription, Google may collect and process information (such as device information, installation data, payment information, and, if you opt in on your device, crash or performance data through Android vitals) under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control this processing.

Permissions

The App requests only the access it needs, and only when you use the related feature. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings; some features may then stop working.

It lets the App show your gallery albums and thumbnails so you can choose photos and videos to move or copy into the Vault, and save them back to your gallery when you restore them. App Lock, PIN, and Recovery Key

ProofFolio lets you protect the App with a master PIN, optional biometric unlock, and a recovery key. The App stores your PIN only in a protected (hashed) form in secure device storage, and shows you the recovery key so that you can keep it safe yourself. The App locks automatically after it is in the background. We do not have a copy of your PIN or recovery key and cannot reset or recover them for you. If you lose both, your protected data cannot be recovered.

How information is used

The limited information described in Section 3 is used only to:

  • Process, verify, and restore your Premium subscription and unlock Premium features;
  • Convert the web page you request into a PDF;
  • Prevent fraud and keep subscription services secure and reliable;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and we do not use the content you store in the App for any purpose.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Information is shared only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers: Google Play (payments) and RevenueCat (subscription management), solely to provide the subscription feature;
  • Websites you choose to open: when you use URL to PDF or open a saved link in your browser;
  • By your own action: when you share a file, evidence item, or case report using Android's share sheet, or open a file in another app on your device, that content goes to the app or person you select. This is controlled by you, and the recipient's own privacy practices apply. Case reports you generate can contain information from your case files, people profiles, and evidence, so please review them before sharing;
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety. Because we do not hold your stored content, we have no such content to disclose.

Retention and deletion

  • Your content on your device: It stays until you delete it. You can delete people profiles, case files (which also deletes the evidence files attached to them), individual evidence items, and Vault items inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes the App's private data from your device. Files you exported or saved outside the App (for example photos restored to your gallery, files shared with other apps, or reports saved elsewhere) are not removed automatically.
  • Subscription records: Purchase and subscription records are kept by Google and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, as described in their policies. To request deletion of RevenueCat-held data, contact us at the email below and we will help submit the request.
  • No account to delete: ProofFolio does not offer account creation, so there is no account or server-side profile for us to delete.

Backups

We do not provide cloud backup or sync. Depending on your device settings, Android's system backup (for example Google backup) may copy parts of an app's data. Encryption keys are held in secure device storage and may not be restored to another device, so you should not rely on system backup to protect your data. Please keep your own copies of important evidence outside the App.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. These include storing text-based records in an encrypted local database (SQLCipher), keeping the encryption key in Android's secure storage, keeping evidence and Vault files in the App's private storage, offering PIN and biometric protection with automatic locking, and using encrypted (HTTPS) connections for subscription services. No method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure. The security of your data also depends on your device (a strong screen lock, up-to-date software, no rooting) and on keeping your PIN and recovery key confidential.

Sensitive and Third-Party Information You Store

ProofFolio may be used to keep records about other people (for example their names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, and details of incidents). You are responsible for making sure that you have the right to collect and store this information and that your use of the App complies with the laws that apply to you. Because this information stays on your device and is not sent to us, you alone control it. Please do not use the App to store unlawful content.

Children's privacy

ProofFolio is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the law of your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App's subscription services, contact us and we will help with removal.

Your Rights and Choices

Because your content stays on your device, you already control it: you can view, edit, export, or delete it inside the App. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR in Europe and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal data held about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. For the limited subscription data described above, contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Uninstalling the App does not cancel a subscription.

Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process subscription-related data because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the Premium features you purchased), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and keeping our services secure.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers, including Google and RevenueCat, may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Please see their privacy policies for details.

Links to Other Websites
