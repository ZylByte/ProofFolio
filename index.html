From first note to finished report.

Five steps, the same every time.

  1. Create a project

    Name the work. It becomes the home for everything related to it.

  2. Add information

    Add people, events and notes, and attach photos, video, audio, documents or saved webpages.

  3. Track progress

    Log what happens on the timeline and turn follow-ups into checklist tasks.

  4. Keep everything organized

    Everything stays linked to its project and its people, so you can find it later.

  5. Generate a report

    Turn what you have gathered into a structured report when you need to summarize or share.

Made to work on your device first.

ProofFolio is offline-first. Organizing your information does not depend on a constant internet connection. Some things are online by nature, and we keep the two clearly apart.

Works without a connection

  • Creating and editing projects, people and events
  • Writing notes and ticking off checklists
  • Reviewing your timeline and keeping files organized on your device

Needs a connection

  • Loading webpages and following links
  • Anything that depends on an online service or website
  • Installing and updating the app from Google Play

What about privacy?

The Private Vault keeps sensitive files in a separate area behind a password. For exactly what ProofFolio stores, which permissions it asks for and how your information is handled, read the Privacy Policy.
